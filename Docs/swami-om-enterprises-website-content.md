## 6. GENERAL SEO NOTES

**Primary keywords to use naturally across the site:**
Shree Swami Samarth products, Swami Samarth devotional items, Akkalkot Maharaj products, Swami Samarth T-shirt, Swami Samarth topi, Swami Samarth dhoti, Swami Samarth kurta, Swami Samarth shawl online, devotional products Maharashtra, wholesale devotional products India

**Suggestions:**
1. Use the product's exact title as the image `alt text` (e.g., alt="Shree Swami Samarth Printed T-Shirt orange cotton") for every product photo — this helps with Google Image search traffic.
2. Add a short FAQ section on product pages (size chart, shipping time, COD availability, return policy) — FAQ content ranks well and answers real buyer questions.
3. Keep meta descriptions under 160 characters so they don't get cut off in Google search results.
4. Since you ship pan-India but focus on Maharashtra, consider a line like "Fast delivery across Maharashtra, shipping available pan-India" on both the homepage and product pages — this helps you rank for local + national searches together.
5. Add your store address with Google Maps embed on the Contact page — this supports local SEO for "Swami Samarth products Akkalkot" type searches.
